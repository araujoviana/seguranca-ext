# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing: static HTML/CSS/JS without build, in `docs/`, served by GitHub Pages. Quiz and test data are generated from `versao1/*.md` by `versao1/gerar-site.sh`; `texto.html` and `checklist.html` are also generated (pandoc), so edits to those must go through the generator or its templates.

## Users

Anyone who pays by tap (NFC card or phone wallet), including people who are not tech-savvy, opening a link on a phone for short sessions. Confirmed in the interview. Secondary readers: the evaluating professor and MackSeguro students, who reach the same pages.

## Product Purpose

Educational trail for the Mackenzie extension project (Grupo 31, 2nd semester 2026): teach people to pay by tap without being scammed or losing money. Eight learning objects: texto, vídeo, caso prático, desafio, quiz (5), checklist, infográfico, teste final (10). Success: a visitor finishes a trail item in a minute or two and leaves with concrete habits (see the texto's seven habits).

## Positioning

The core claim a neighbour could not copy without the research: the NFC technology is well protected (one-time code per purchase); the loss comes from use (lost card, crowded places, wrong amount on the display, and above all the "broken" card-machine scam). Content is centered on that distinction, with interactive objects (case with a running loss counter, spot-the-errors scene).

## Operating Context

Prototype/showcase for the Etapa 2 partial delivery (30/10/2026); final delivery 12/11/2026 with a 5-minute authored video. The site is independent of the MackSeguro platform (React app, content in `src/data/mock.ts`); the final content goes there as TypeScript (`mackseguro/questoes-pagamentos-aproximacao.ts`). Badge and certificate are generated by the platform, not by this site. PDFs and a PNG infographic are downloadable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Role of the site: independent showcase/prototype, not a visual clone of MackSeguro (confirmed).
- Keep all 8 objects; cut long copy, repeated notes and over-explained text, not objects (confirmed).
- Language: pt-BR.
- Interactive pieces: quiz, teste final (70% pass), caso prático (decisions with loss counter), desafio (tap risky passages, with decoys), checklist (state saved in the browser only).
- Video is not recorded yet; only script and three Manim animations exist.
- Two authors: Matheus Gabriel Viana Araujo (RA 10420444), Danilo Oliveira Santos (RA 10410905).

## Brand Commitments

User-stated binding constraints on the site: monochrome look, no dark mode, no "AI-slop" / generic dev-template feel, a better typeface than the system font, diagrams in the caso prático, a consistent high-quality design language.

## Evidence on Hand

Texto with sources (`versao1/1-texto.md`), roteiro, 3 rendered animations (`docs/assets/video/`), infographic (`docs/downloads/8-infografico.*`), 15 questions with per-alternative explanations. No testimonials, usage data, or recorded video; do not fabricate them. The limit for contactless without password is sourced from 2020 articles; the text warns it varies by issuer.

## Product Principles

1. Truth over fear: show that the technology is safe and the risk is in behaviour.
2. One idea per screen; short copy that a phone reader finishes.
3. Learning by doing: the interactive objects carry the teaching, text supports them.
4. Every wrong answer teaches something specific.
5. Disclose AI assistance honestly, in one place, without cluttering every page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Non-expert and older users are in scope: large tap targets (44px+), readable body size, high contrast, keyboard operability, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, no color-only meaning (important in a monochrome site where right/wrong must not rely on green/red alone).
